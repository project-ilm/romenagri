## Open issues for linguists

- Phonetic fidelity and dialects: Kashmiri has vowels (ɨ, ə, ɔ) that Devanagari lacks; Sharada signs for them are unmatched.
- Research needed: Kashmiri vowel extensions; which Devanagari extension to use.
